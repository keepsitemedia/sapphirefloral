Never do these

Read once

Each of these can get a profile demoted or suspended, and getting reinstated is slow and miserable.

  • Do not add keywords to your business name “Sapphire Stem Floral Design” is the name. Not “Sapphire Stem Floral Design | Big Timber Montana Wedding Florist.” This is the most common suspension cause, and competitors do report it.
  • Do not list an address you do not staff No PO boxes, no a friend’s shop, no coworking address. If you work from home and would rather not publish it, hide it and use a service area — that is what the setting is for.
  • Do not buy reviews or swap them with other vendors Google detects clustered reviews from unrelated accounts and removes them, and the profile takes the damage. Ten real reviews outrank fifty suspicious ones.
  • Do not create a second profile One business, one profile. Duplicates split your reviews and photos across two listings and Google may suspend both.

Tick the boxes as you go — the count at the top updates. The list does not save when you close the page, so work through a section at a time.
