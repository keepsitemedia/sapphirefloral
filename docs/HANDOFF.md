# Your website — how to run it

Everything on the site that changes often, you can change yourself. This is the
whole manual. Nothing here needs a developer.

---

## 1. Logging in

Go to **sapphirestemfloral.com/admin**.

You will get an email invitation to set a password (it comes from DecapBridge,
the service that handles logins). Accept it once, then afterwards you can sign in
with your email and password, or with your Google account.

Bookmark that address. It is the only page you need.

---

## 2. Adding a wedding to the portfolio

1. Click **Portfolio**, then **New Photo**.
2. **Title** — a short name for the photo, like "Riverside arch". Only you and
   the sort order use this; it is not printed on the page.
3. **Photo** — upload the image. Straight from your phone or your photographer's
   gallery is fine.
4. **Describe the photo** — this one matters. Write it the way you would describe
   the picture to someone on the phone: *"Cascading bouquet of purple orchids and
   red roses held against a lace gown by the river."* Two things depend on it:
   Google reads it to understand what the photo shows, and anyone using a screen
   reader hears it instead of seeing the image. Name the flowers if you know
   them — that is exactly what brides search for.
5. **Photographer credit** — optional, prints under the photo. See section 5.
6. **Show on homepage** — turn on for your four to six strongest images. The
   homepage shows the first six of these, in sort order.
7. **Sort order** — lower numbers come first. Leave gaps (10, 20, 30) so you can
   slot a new photo between two old ones without renumbering everything.
8. **Category** — wedding, event, personals (bouquets, corsages, boutonnieres),
   or installation (arches, large pieces).
9. Click **Publish**.

**Give it about two minutes.** Publishing rebuilds the site, so the change is not
instant. Refresh the page after a couple of minutes and it will be there.

Keep uploads under about 8 MB each. Bigger files still work, they just make the
site slower to update over time.

---

## 3. Changing prices

Click **À la carte prices → Price menu**. Every item is on one screen. Edit a
price, add an item, drag to reorder, then **Publish**. Same two-minute wait.

The prices show as "Starting at $X" on the weddings page. The section headings
(personals, ceremony, reception) come from the **Section** dropdown on each item.

---

## 4. The banner

The blue strip across the top of every page — "Now booking 2026 & 2027 weddings
and events" — lives in **Site settings → Booking banner**. Update the years as
the seasons roll over.

## 6. What not to do

- Don't edit anything outside **Portfolio**, **À la carte prices**, and **Site
  settings**. Those three are yours. Anything else, call us.
- Don't delete the **Describe the photo** text on an existing entry. The site is
  set up to refuse to build without it, which is deliberate — it is what keeps
  the site readable to Google and to screen readers.
- Don't worry about breaking it. Every change is saved in version history and can
  be rolled back.
